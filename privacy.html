Privacy policy

Tore Bey is a personal assistant used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google accounts, with the owner's consent.
  • Email data is used only to carry out the owner's requests: reading, searching, labelling, moving to Trash and sending mail.
  • Data is stored only on machines the owner controls. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tore Bey's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
